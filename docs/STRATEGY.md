## 5. The 30-day content system

**Focus:** LinkedIn + X hard (that's where US/UK/EU clients & recruiters are). Repost to WhatsApp Status + IG. One hour/day max: create once, repurpose.

**Pillars (rotate):** Work 40% · Process 20% · Teach 20% · Build-in-public 10% · Offer 10%.

**Voice rules:** short lines. Specific, not hype. Show the work first, talk second. Never "excited to announce." Nigerian-direct is fine and distinctive — don't sand it off.

**Universal CTA bank (rotate, don't beg):**
- *If you're launching something, my DMs are open.*
- *Building a product and need it to actually ship? Let's talk.*
- *Full case study on my site — link in bio.*

## 6. Fast-money path (do this alongside content)

Content compounds over *months*. If cash is urgent *now*:
1. **Upwork + Contra profiles** this week — same positioning, link the portfolio, apply to 5 quality jobs/day.
2. **Targeted cold outreach** — small businesses / founders with ugly or broken sites; send one specific, non-generic message + one relevant case-study link.
3. **Referrals** — WhatsApp Status + tell everyone you know you're taking client work. Warm leads close fastest.
The portfolio is the closer in every one of these. That's why Phase 0–1 come first.
